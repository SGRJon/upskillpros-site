WHY JOB SCOUT

Most free job tools are paid for by someone else. This one isn't.

Job sites earn their money from employers, ads, or subscriptions, so your attention and your profile are part of the deal. Job Scout runs on your own computer and costs nothing to run, so it doesn't need anything from you.

01

No ads, no recruiters paying to reach you, and nobody buying access to your profile. Nobody gets access to you through Job Scout.

03

WHY IT ISN'T A WEBSITE

Because a website would need your data to pay for itself.

A hosted job tool has to pay for servers and AI for every user, so it earns that back with ads, subscriptions, or by selling access to you. Job Scout runs on your computer and uses your own Claude plan if you have one. That keeps your resume and job search private, keeps it free, and means nobody has to sell your attention to keep the lights on.

It also lets Job Scout do things a website can't, like read the job-alert emails already in your inbox and spot your "thank you for applying" confirmations.

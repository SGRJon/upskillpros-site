Case Study 01

A treasury that ran on manual entry now runs itself

The problem

A statewide athletic association ran its finances by hand. Transactions keyed into spreadsheets, checks written manually, and tax season meaning weeks of assembling records for the CPA.

What I built

  • A multi-tenant financial operations platform that ingests transaction data automatically, replacing manual entry
  • Check printing directly from the system
  • CPA-ready workbooks generated from live data at tax time
  • Role-based access, audit trails, and tenant-level data isolation, because financial data deserves nothing less

What changed

Hours of monthly manual work eliminated, records that reconcile themselves, and a treasurer who runs the whole operation without calling me. The first organization's build now serves additional organizations on the same codebase. That's the multi-tenant bet paying off.

Case Study 02

A peer recognition platform, built from scratch

The problem

Companies pay per-user monthly fees for employee recognition software, then get charged the same rate for every feature they never use. I believed the core of it, points, recognition, and celebration, could be built better and priced fairer.

What I built

  • Peer-to-peer recognition with points, a live activity feed, and GIF celebration
  • Multi-recipient recognition, hashtag values, and a completion moment that makes giving feel good
  • Multi-tenant architecture with configurable branding, one codebase, many companies
  • Light and dark modes, mobile-first responsive design

Status

Live in production with demo data, in pilot conversations with its first prospective customer. All names shown are demo accounts.

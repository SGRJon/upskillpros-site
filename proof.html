CASE STUDY 01 · FINANCIAL OPERATIONS

A treasury that ran on manual entry now runs itself.

A statewide athletic association ran its finances by hand. Transactions keyed into spreadsheets, checks written manually, and tax season meaning weeks of assembling records for the CPA. Last season, the mess had a price tag: a $7,000 CPA bill, driven largely by records that arrived scattered and inconsistent.

WHAT I BUILT
  • A multi-tenant financial operations platform that ingests transaction data automatically, replacing manual entry
  • Check printing directly from the system
  • CPA-ready workbooks generated from live data, built to cut that $7,000 bill by thousands this year
  • Role-based access, audit trails, and tenant-level data isolation, because financial data deserves nothing less
WHAT CHANGED

Hours of monthly manual work eliminated, records that stay reconciled all year, real cost reduction at tax time, and a treasurer who runs the whole operation without calling me. The first organization's build now serves additional organizations on the same codebase.
